Privacy Policy

Last updated September 19, 2026.

Seamar (Seamarbroken S.A.) respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it is handled.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, organization (if given), and message. We do not collect any other personal information through this site, and we do not use cookies or tracking scripts.

How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties, except as described below.

Third-party processing

Contact form submissions are delivered to us using FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email relay service. FormSubmit processes the content of your submission only to route it to our inbox; you can review its own privacy practices at formsubmit.co/privacy-policy. This website is hosted on Cloudflare's network, which may log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes, consistent with Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Data retention

We retain inquiry correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to address the matter raised and to maintain our own business records, after which it may be deleted.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To do so, or with any other privacy question, contact us at seamarlogistic.la@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the site operates. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
