What We Do

Services

Seamar provides independent technical and regulatory advisory to the maritime industry, informed by direct experience across vessel operations, inspection and surveying, flag-state administration, and the international regulatory process.

Principal Advisory Areas
01 · Investigation

Marine Casualty & Accident Investigation

Seamar provides independent investigation into marine casualties, working from first response through findings — informed by direct experience as a flag-state Maritime Accident Investigator for the Panama Maritime Authority, subsequent incident-investigation and management-system audit work in a corporate setting, and IMO-recognized accident-investigation training.

02 · Disputes

Expert Witness & Technical Opinion

Seamar provides independent technical opinion for maritime disputes, for legal teams that need a technically grounded, defensible assessment — drawing on direct experience serving as an expert witness in international maritime legal matters.

03 · Compliance

Flag-State & Regulatory Advisory

Seamar advises shipowners, managers, and flag administrations on regulatory compliance and engagement, informed by direct experience as a flag-state and port-state inspector and nearly six years representing Panama before the International Maritime Organization — first-hand understanding of how international maritime regulation, including SOLAS, MARPOL, STCW, ISM, and ISPS, is developed and applied.

04 · Inspection

Vetting & Inspection Support

Seamar supports vetting inspections, P&I-related surveys, and on/off-hire inspections, backed by OCIMF-accredited tanker inspector and licensed SIRE/OCIMF vetting inspector credentials, with inspection experience across oil and chemical tankers, container vessels, tugboats, fishing vessels, and coastal vessels and barges.

Additional Capabilities

Supporting Expertise

  • ISM/ISPS/ISO Audit & Quality Management

    Lead Auditor certified across ISO 9001, ISM, ISPS, ISO 14001, and OH&S, with experience developing quality manuals and management systems.

  • Fire Safety & Life-Saving Systems Compliance

    An unusually deep certification base in fire-suppression systems and NFPA standards, applied to compliance review.

  • Decarbonization & Emissions Advisory

    Certified in EEXI compliance and carbon intensity reduction, supporting owners in meeting decarbonization requirements.

  • Maritime Training & Curriculum Development

    Former lecturer in maritime safety, cruise business management, and naval technology at two Panamanian universities; available for seafarer and maritime-personnel training.

  • Marine Insurance & P&I Technical Advisory

    A licensed insurance broker with specific training in hull & machinery and P&I marine insurance.

  • IMO and Flag Administration Engagement

    Direct experience representing a flag state before the IMO and affiliated bodies, for organizations engaging with international regulatory bodies.

Seamar serves clients internationally, with experience spanning Panama, the Americas, Europe, and Asia.
